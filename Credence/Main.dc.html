Why Rhodopotash

Feeds the crop. Leaves the soil healthier.

Description

Red-algae potash with trace Sulphur

  • Potash derived from sustainably harvested Australian red algae.
  • Contains trace Sulphur for optimum plant nutrition.
  • ECO-friendly and completely safe for all soils.
  • Controls opening and closing of stomata.

Plant benefits

Stronger, more resilient crops

  • Acts as a source of organic Potash
  • Supports carbohydrate formation
  • Improves yield
  • Increases tolerance to drought
  • Enhances nutrient absorption
  • Promotes balanced growth

Soil benefits

Healthy soil, less chemical Potash

  • Does not degrade soil health
  • ECO-friendly and safe
  • Passes through drip irrigation
  • Helps cut down on chemical Potash
  • Does not interfere with soil microbes
  • Improves shelf life of yield
